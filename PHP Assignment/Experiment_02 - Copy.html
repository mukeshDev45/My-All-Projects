Recipe: Maggi

  • Ingredients
    • 1 packet Maggi
    • 2 cups water
    • 1 tsp oil
    • Vegetables
      • Onion
      • Tomato
      • Capsicum
  • Steps
    1. Boil water in a pan
    2. Add vegetables and cook for 2 minutes
    3. Add Maggi noodles and tastemaker
    4. Cook for 3–4 minutes
    5. Serve hot!
